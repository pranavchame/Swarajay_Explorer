Our research approach.

The published catalogue contains 234 photographed forts and 179 source-referenced map locations. Each profile links its research and photo credits. Entries without a verified photograph are kept outside the public catalogue. Exact dates, elevations and routes remain unknown where the evidence is insufficient.

Historical associations do not establish a fort’s builder or an uninterrupted period of possession. Trek grades are provisional source interpretations; photographs and coordinates have not been independently authenticated by a field survey. Read the references and check current access locally.

Explore with respect.

  • Check weather, permissions and access before travelling.
  • Use established paths and qualified local guidance for exposed or unfamiliar terrain.
  • Carry drinking water, leave enough daylight and confirm return transport.
  • Respect sacred spaces, local communities and fragile masonry.
  • Carry waste back. Never carve names or remove historical material.

This is an independent educational project with no official affiliation to heritage authorities.
