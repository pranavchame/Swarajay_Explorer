This is not a Creative Commons licence or an assertion that the permission agreement was independently audited.

No photographer identity is fabricated. No Trekshitiz architectural layout maps are included.

## Historical text

The fort database retains per-entry references. Editorial summaries, structured catalogue facts, brief source notes and attributed owner-authorized Trekshitiz extracts are distinguished in the records. The 51 new timeline chapters adapt Wikipedia source accounts: citation markers and parenthetical material are omitted, selected passages shortened, and contextual leads added where necessary. The adapted text is available under CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Each chapter links its source article and contributor history for attribution. Historical interpretations and dates may differ between sources.
