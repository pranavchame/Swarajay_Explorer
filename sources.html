Historical and geographical sources

Each fort page links its own source account. The catalogue uses structured geographical facts from the English Wikipedia fort articles, with source URLs preserved in the fort database. Historical entries combine original editorial summaries, short encyclopaedic notes and attributed Trekshitiz guide extracts used under the permission stated by the project owner. Exact tenures remain unverified where the sources do not establish them.

  • Wikipedia — List of forts in Maharashtra
  • UNESCO — Maratha Military Landscapes of India
  • Trekshitiz — additional field and trekking research

Review date: 14 November 2026. Individual records distinguish unknown details. Original ZIP photographs with unclear reuse rights or mismatched fort identities were not republished.

Documentary photographs

Wikimedia photographs retain their individual licences. Trekshitiz photographs are included under full permission confirmed by the project owner on 15 November 2026; no Creative Commons licence is asserted for those images. Where the source does not name an individual photographer, the archive is credited without inventing a name. Photographs are resized and converted to WebP. The applicable original license continues to apply. Image identity evidence is recorded in the gallery manifest. Article-linked categories and explicit fort-name associations are used; photographs have not been authenticated by a field survey. All gallery credits. Download structured credits.
