THE STORY BEHIND THE STONE

Why Visapur Fort
matters.

Visapur rises beside Lohagad and belongs to the same strategic landscape above the passes near Lonavala. The fort’s large plateau preserves fortification remains and water structures. British forces took the fort during the campaign of 1818.
