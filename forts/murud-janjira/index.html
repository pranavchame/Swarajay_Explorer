THE STORY BEHIND THE STONE

Why Murud-Janjira
matters.

Janjira was the sea stronghold of the Siddis. Its offshore position and substantial defences enabled it to resist repeated efforts by neighbouring powers to take it. It represents a distinct political and maritime history alongside the Maratha coastal forts.

MAHARASHTRA TOURISM · BROCHURE GUIDE

Murud-Janjira: heritage & nearby places

Murud-Janjira was a major island stronghold of the Siddis. Its protected entrance, bastions, freshwater supply and large cannons illustrate how a self-contained sea fort could resist repeated attacks.

Landmarks to look for

  • Main entrance
  • Kalalbangdi, Chavri and Landa Kasam cannons
  • Freshwater wells
  • Ramparts and internal remains

Places in the wider area

  • Dutta Mandir
  • Eidgah
  • Nawab Palace
  • Murud Beach
  • Khokri tombs at Rajapuri
  • Rajapuri sunset point
  • Amboli Dam
  • Garambi Waterfall
  • Sawatkada Waterfall
  • Kashid Beach

Brochure activity ideas: Explore the entrance, cannons, passages and views.
