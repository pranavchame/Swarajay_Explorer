THE STORY BEHIND THE STONE

Why Shivneri
matters.

Shivneri is the birthplace of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. The fort guards the area around Junnar, a settlement with a much older commercial and political history. Its sequence of gates shows the value of defence in depth.

A birthplace protected by successive gateways ＋

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was born at Shivneri on 19 February 1630. The fort above Junnar is closely connected with Rajmata Jijabai and Shahaji Raje. Its place in Maratha memory comes from this association with the beginning of his life, rather than from being a later capital of Swarajya.

Steep sides and a sequence of seven gateways shape the approach. The Shivai Devi shrine and surviving water-storage structures connect the military setting with religious and everyday life. Maharashtra Tourism describes rocky ground and warns that rainy-season paths can be slippery; present access and the chosen route should still be checked locally.

The Deccan before Swarajya

Jijabai and the Bhonsle household

Jijabai, was the mother of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, founder of the Maratha Kingdom. She was a daughter of Lakhuji Jadhav of Sindkhed Raja. He belonged to the lineage of Devagiri Yadavas. Jijabai was born on 12 January 1598, to Mhalasabai Jadhav and Lakhuji Jadhav at Sindkhed Raja in present-day Buldhana district of Maharashtra. Jijabai was married at an early age to Shahaji, son of Maloji Bhosale of Verul village, a military commander serving under the Ahmadnagar Sultanate. She was a skilled horse rider, sword fighter, academician and administrator. Scholar James Laine states that Shivaji Maharaj was imbued with the dream of re-establishing a Hindu kingdom by his mother, Jijabai, whose ancestors the Yadavas held the sovereignty of the Deccan for 150 years pre Islamic invasions. She taught him the nuances of tactics, administration and politics right from an early age. She died on 17 June 1674 at Pachad village near Raigad Fort. This was only eleven days after the coronation of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

MAHARASHTRA TOURISM · BROCHURE GUIDE

Shivneri: heritage & nearby places

Shivneri is the birthplace of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. Its successive gates, water storage and Shivai Devi shrine make it an important place for understanding his early life and the defensive architecture around Junnar.

Landmarks to look for

  • Seven gates
  • Badami Talav
  • Shivai Devi Temple
  • Jijabai and young Shivaji statues
  • Ganga and Jamuna springs

Places in the wider area

  • Leopard rescue centre
  • Malshej Ghat
