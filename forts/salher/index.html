Salher and a major field victory

Salher stands in the Baglan region of present-day Nashik district. Maratha forces captured the fort in 1671. Mughal forces subsequently attempted to recover it. The campaign led to a major battle in 1672. Moropant Pingle and Prataprao Gujar are associated with the Maratha relief effort. The Maratha victory lifted the pressure on the besieged stronghold. It demonstrated capacity in open campaigning as well as fort defence. Baglan’s routes made the surrounding region strategically important. Salher’s story belongs to a network that includes neighbouring Mulher. Its importance does not depend on exaggerated troop or casualty figures.

Read full story ＋

Salher’s commanding position in Baglan gave it a place in the contest for routes linking the northern Deccan and western India. Maratha forces captured the fort in 1671. Mughal attempts to recover it brought a siege and then a major confrontation in 1672. The successful Maratha relief is associated with the leadership of Moropant Pingle and Prataprao Gujar. The result secured the position at a significant moment in the expansion of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj’s power.

The campaign is important because it broadens the familiar picture of Maratha warfare. Fortified heights and rapid movement were important, but the victory at Salher also involved substantial forces operating outside the fort. A garrison’s ability to hold a position and a relief army’s ability to reach it were related parts of the same operation. Reading only the walls would miss that wider battlefield and the routes through which supplies and troops moved.

Neighbouring strongholds, including Mulher, help place Salher within a regional system. Control of one hill did not automatically guarantee control of every valley or road. Garrisons, provisions and the ability to respond to an opposing army all mattered. This is why the region remained contested after individual victories and why possession dates must be distinguished from the date of a single battle.

Accounts of Salher sometimes offer very large totals for troops, prisoners or casualties. This guide does not repeat figures that have not been carefully reconciled across sources. The strategic result is clear without them: the Maratha forces overcame the besiegers and reinforced their position in Baglan. For visitors, the historical importance lies in the coordination required to turn a threatened stronghold into a successful regional operation, as well as in the exceptional landscape of the fort itself.

Salher: heritage & nearby places

Salher rises above the Baglan landscape and is presented as the highest fort in the Sahyadris. Its commanding views and association with conflicts during Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj’s reign make it important to the region’s military history.

Landmarks to look for

  • Views of Salota
  • Salher hills
  • Sunrise and sunset viewpoints
