THE STORY BEHIND THE STONE

Why Belapur Fort
matters.

The citadel of the fort was located at a height of 75 ft. The walls were strong and had 5 bastions and 20 canons.Chronology of events:31st March, 1737: Narayan Joshi laid a siege at this fort and brought the fort under Maratha rule on April 22, 1737.
