THE STORY BEHIND THE STONE

Why Bandra Fort
matters.

The Portuguese built this coastal fortification at Bandra in the seventeenth century. Its position overlooked approaches around Mahim Bay. The surviving remains are better known today as Bandra Fort.

Also known as Castella de Aguada

Rulers & historical periods ＋

Maratha, Portuguese, British / East India Company
